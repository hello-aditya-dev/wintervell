## Hero

**WinterVell — Turn any website into a professional audit, proposal,
and sales opportunity.**

White-label AI Website Audit and Agency Sales Platform for agencies,
freelancers, and MSPs. Run a structured six-category audit, generate a
branded PDF report and proposal, and track the deal through a
twelve-stage pipeline — all under your agency's brand.

## The problem

Agencies struggle to turn "let me look at your website" into a paid
engagement. The audit takes hours in spreadsheets and screenshots, the
report looks inconsistent, the proposal is a copy-paste job, and
follow-up falls through the cracks. Prospects ghost because the
deliverable does not feel professional.

## The solution

WinterVell compresses the prospect-to-proposal workflow into one
platform:

1. Capture the prospect and their website.
2. Run a structured six-category audit (Technical, SEO, Accessibility,
   Conversion & UX, Trust, AI & Search Visibility).
3. Review findings, mark false positives, verify, and approve.
4. Generate a branded PDF report with selectable text, page numbers,
   and your logo.
5. Generate a proposal with scope, deliverables, pricing, payment
   schedule, and acceptance criteria.
6. Share on your custom domain, track engagement, and convert the
   prospect into a pipeline opportunity.

Every client-facing surface is white-label. The product never implies a
real audit ran when it did not, and never implies a real integration is
live when it is mocked.

## Features

- Six-category audit engine with a twelve-stage pipeline.
- Nine-category transparent, reproducible, versioned scoring.
- Structured evidence model (URL, selector, screenshot, raw evidence,
  business consequence, recommended action, suggested service).
- Manual review workflow with false-positive marking and approval
  gating.
- Report builder with section reordering, live preview, and branded PDF
  generation.
- Proposal generator with implementation roadmap (Immediate / 30 / 60 /
  90-day).
- Twelve-stage opportunity pipeline with drag-and-drop and stage
  history.
- Privacy-conscious report analytics (first-viewed, last-viewed, view
  count, CTA clicks, downloads).
- Full client-facing white-labelling: logo, colours, fonts, custom
  domain, sender identity.
- AI provider abstraction: OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic, mock;
  bring-your-own-key.
- NextAuth v4 authentication, RBAC, tenant isolation, audit logging.
- Northstar Digital demo organization (fictional, clearly labelled).

## Tiers (AppSumo stacking framing)

> WinterVell's WV-CSL licence prohibits resale of the source code.
> AppSumo codes grant a **licence** to operate WinterVell — they do
> not grant resale or redistribution rights. The source-code tiers
> grant a **source licence for internal use**, not a resale right.

| Code stack | Tier granted | Source | Deployments | White-label (client) | White-label (admin) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 code | Hosted (LTD) | No | 1 hosted | Yes | No |
| 3 codes | Hosted (LTD, higher limits) | No | 1 hosted | Yes | No |
| 5 codes | Agency Source (LTD) | Yes | 1 production | Yes | Configurable |
| Custom | Studio | Yes | Up to 5 production | Yes | Full |

Studio is not sold as a stackable AppSumo code; it is offered as a
custom arrangement via direct contact. Sublicensing requires separate
written authorization under WV-CSL.

## Limitations (honest, AppSumo-appropriate)

- v0.1.0 is the foundation release; the eighteen-module product
  implementation is in progress per the published roadmap. Buyers
  should review [`../ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md) before purchase.
- Automated findings are indicative, not definitive. WinterVell does
  not certify WCAG or legal compliance and does not guarantee SEO or
  ranking improvements.
- The product is white-label-first on client-facing surfaces; admin-area
  white-labelling depends on tier.
- WinterVell is **not open source**. AppSumo codes grant a licence to
  operate the product; they do not grant resale, sublicensing, or
  public-publication rights.
- Formal trademark clearance for "WinterVell" is recommended before
  major commercial investment.
- Legal documents are templates; review with a qualified lawyer before
  commercial distribution.
