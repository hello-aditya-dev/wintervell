## Listing description (approximately 1,000 characters)

WinterVell is a proprietary AI Website Audit and Agency Sales Platform
built for web-design, SEO, marketing, and conversion agencies. It turns
any website into a structured audit, a polished client-facing report, a
service proposal, and a tracked sales opportunity — one workflow from
prospect capture to closed client project. Built on Next.js 16, React
19, TypeScript, Prisma (PostgreSQL), NextAuth v4, and a provider-agnostic
AI abstraction, WinterVell ships with a complete legal and
documentation foundation (WV-CSL v1.0 licence, provenance, conversion
audit, security disclosure, EULA template, sale due-diligence pack) and
a Northstar Digital demonstration organization. Three commercial tiers
— Hosted, Agency Source, and Studio — cover single agencies through
multi-brand studios. The product is white-label-first: every
client-facing surface carries the agency's brand. v0.1.0 is the
foundation release; the eighteen-module product roadmap is published
and in progress.

## Feature bullets

- Six-category audit engine: Technical, SEO, Accessibility, Conversion
  & UX, Trust & Commercial Readiness, AI & Search Visibility.
- Nine-category transparent, reproducible, versioned scoring.
- Twelve-stage audit pipeline with per-stage retry and partial results.
- Structured evidence model (category, severity, confidence, URL,
  selector, screenshot, raw evidence, explanation, business consequence,
  recommended action, estimated effort, suggested service, verification
  flag, timestamp, tool/provider).
- Manual review workflow: add, edit, mark false positive, verify,
  exclude, approve.
- Report builder with section enable/disable, reordering, live preview,
  and branded PDF generation with selectable text and page numbers.
- Proposal generator with scope, deliverables, exclusions, assumptions,
  pricing, payment schedule, acceptance criteria, and signatures.
- Implementation roadmap (Immediate / 30 / 60 / 90-day) with
  dependencies, complexity, priority, and expected impact.
- Twelve-stage opportunity pipeline with drag-and-drop and stage
  history.
- Privacy-conscious report analytics (first-viewed, last-viewed, view
  count, CTA clicks, download activity).
- Full client-facing white-labelling: logo, colours, typography, custom
  domain, sender identity, default disclaimers.
- AI provider abstraction: OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic, and a built-in
  mock provider; bring-your-own-key; token and cost logging.
- NextAuth v4 authentication, RBAC, tenant isolation, rate limiting,
  and append-only audit logging.
- Northstar Digital demonstration organization with five fictional,
  clearly-labelled prospects.
- SSRF-protected audit worker architecture (designed and documented).
